# Retention and purge

A daily procrastinate task on the `default` queue, `retention.purge`, applies these rules in small batches (at most 5000 rows per statement, repeated until done) so it never holds long locks. It runs with `SET LOCAL motionile.allow_purge = 'on'`, the only way to delete from append-only tables. Every run writes one `audit_log` entry with the counts it removed.

Indian law sets the floor for financial records: books of account and invoices are kept for at least 8 years (Companies Act, 2013, section 128; CGST Act, 2017, section 36 requires 72 months after the annual return due date). Billing data below is therefore kept for 8 years after the end of the financial year it belongs to.

## Rules per table

| Table                                                                 | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `users` (soft-deleted)                                                | 30 days after `deleted_at`: delete the user's storage objects, then hard-delete the row and everything that cascades. If the user has billing history (`orders`, `payments`, `credit_ledger`), anonymize instead: clear `name`, `avatar_storage_key`, delete contacts, credentials, OAuth identities, sessions, preferences, memories, RAG data, voices, skills and connected accounts, and keep the row with `status = 'deleted'` until the billing retention ends. |
| `users` (never verified)                                              | Accounts still `pending_verification` 7 days after creation are deleted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `sessions`                                                            | Delete 30 days after `absolute_expires_at`, or 30 days after `revoked_at`, whichever comes first. The extra 30 days keep evidence for reuse investigations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `idempotency_records`                                                 | Delete when `expires_at` has passed (24 hours after creation by default). In-progress records whose `locked_until` passed more than 1 hour ago are deleted too.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `job_events`                                                          | Delete 90 days after `created_at`, using `ix_job_events_created_at`. Jobs keep their final status and summary; the event stream is only needed for live progress and debugging. Deleting a job or its user removes its events through the foreign key, which the append-only trigger allows because it is a referential action.                                                                                                                                      |
| `webhook_events`                                                      | After 90 days, replace `payload` with `'{}'` for `processed` and `ignored` events and keep the row for deduplication. Delete rows after 8 years. `failed` events keep their payload until resolved.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `audit_log`                                                           | Keep 2 years online. Older partitions are exported to the archive bucket as compressed JSON lines and dropped. Entries about billing actions are exported, not deleted, for 8 years.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `usage_records`                                                       | Keep 2 years online, then roll up into monthly per-user and per-provider totals in the archive and drop the partition.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| `credit_lots`, `credit_reservations`, `billing_reconciliation_runs`   | Keep 8 years after the financial year ends; they explain the ledger. Reconciliation reports in storage follow the same rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `credit_ledger`                                                       | Keep 8 years after the financial year ends. Never deleted earlier, even when the user is deleted.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `orders`, `payments`, `refunds`, `subscriptions`, `billing_customers` | Keep 8 years after the financial year ends.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `assets` (pending)                                                    | Uploads still `pending` 24 hours after creation are deleted with their objects.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `renders` (`source = 'testbed'`)                                      | Delete 14 days after `completed_at` with their renditions, subtitle tracks and assets. Renders of jobs and MCP submissions follow their job.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| `work/` objects                                                       | Stage checkpoints and intermediate files under `work/renders/<id>/` are removed by a bucket lifecycle rule 7 days after creation and by the render when it finishes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `tts_cache`                                                           | `media.prune_tts_cache` deletes entries not used for 90 days with their audio. Deleting a cloned voice deletes its entries at once.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `music_tracks`                                                        | Kept while the row exists. Deactivating a track stops new use; licence records are never deleted while a render that used the track exists.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| `voice_consents`                                                      | Kept as long as the cloned voice exists, plus 3 years after the voice is deleted, as evidence of consent. Deleting the voice sets `voice_id` to null; the consent recording stays until the 3 years pass, then the row and the recording are deleted. A user deletion cascades to the row.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `mcp_grants`, `api_keys`                                              | Delete 90 days after `expires_at` or `revoked_at`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `eval_runs`                                                           | Delete 1 year after `finished_at` (or `started_at` for runs that never finished), except runs whose `prompt_version_ids` contain a currently active prompt version, which are kept while that version is active.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `storyboards`, `scenes`                                               | Follow their job: removed with it by the foreign key cascade. Their assets are removed with the job's other assets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `share_links`                                                         | Delete 30 days after `expires_at` or `revoked_at`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `jobs` and their content                                              | Kept while the user's account exists. Failed and cancelled jobs older than 180 days are deleted with their assets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Monthly partitioning

These tables grow without bound. Each is converted to a range-partitioned table on `created_at`, with one partition per month, when it reaches the threshold below or one year after launch, whichever comes first.

| Table           | Threshold       | Expected growth driver                |
| --------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `audit_log`     | 20 million rows | Logins, admin actions, billing events |
| `usage_records` | 20 million rows | Several rows per generation stage     |
| `job_events`    | 50 million rows | Dozens of events per job              |
| `credit_ledger` | 10 million rows | Several rows per job and purchase     |

Conversion plan, executed as one expand/contract sequence per table:

1. Create the partitioned table `<name>_partitioned` with the same columns, primary key `(id, created_at)` (the partition key must be part of every unique constraint), the same checks, indexes and the append-only trigger.
2. Create partitions for every month that has data and the next three months; a monthly task keeps three future partitions ready.
3. Copy rows in batches by `created_at`, oldest first.
4. In one short transaction: copy the remaining rows, rename the tables, and swap foreign keys that point at the table (none of the four have incoming foreign keys).
5. Drop the old table in the next release.

`credit_ledger.idempotency_key` uniqueness cannot be global on a partitioned table; after conversion it becomes unique together with `created_at`, and the ledger service checks the key inside its transaction, which already locks the user's wallet row.
